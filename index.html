Our story
where omega comes from

      

        
With our dads both being pastors, we spent a majority of our time in the church. This gave us a deep love for serving others and pursuing excellence in everything we do. Whether we were planning events, creating social media content, or simply finding ways to make people feel seen and valued, creativity and serving others have always been at the heart of who we are.

        
Our business is named after our nana, Shirley Omega Ford. Our childhood was spent at her house making home videos, doing photoshoots, silly skits, and anything that involved a camera. Looking back, we’re so thankful to have those moments preserved, and they’re a big part of what inspired our passion for helping others capture memories they too will treasure for a lifetime.

        
Today, our passion is creating content that feels authentic, intentional, and timeless. Whether we’re capturing your wedding day, bringing your business to life through social media, or telling the story behind your brand or event, our goal is always the same: to create content that you can relive, share, and cherish.

        
Now that you know a little about us, our background, and the heart behind what we do, we can’t wait to get to know you and tell your story.
